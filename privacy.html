Privacy

This is a starter template describing how this version of the site works. If you change what the site collects, update this page. Consider having a lawyer review it before you promote the site widely, especially if minors may use it.

Browsing the site

Scores, schedules, rosters and standings need no account. Your favorite teams, your theme choice and some recently loaded data are saved in your own browser and are not sent to us.

Fan Talk accounts

If you create a Fan Talk account we store your username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password and recovery code, your favorite teams, who you've blocked, and the messages you post. We don't ask for your real name, and an email address is optional. If you choose to add one so you can get password reset codes by email, we store it (after you confirm it with a code), it is never shown to other people, and you can remove it any time. Those emails are sent through an email delivery service, which receives your address and the message for that purpose. Please don't use your real name as a username.

Recruiting accounts

Recruiting is a locked area for players and college coaches. To ask for access you give us details so a person can check who you are. We keep them until you delete them or an administrator removes them.

  • Recruiting has its own account, separate from Fan Talk. We store the email address you sign up with, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password and recovery code, and when you joined. We do not check that you own the email address, and it is never shown to other players or coaches. We use it only to send you messages about your account: a password reset code if you ask for one, and a note when your request is approved or not approved. These emails are sent through an email delivery service, which receives your address and the message for that purpose.
  • Players give their name, birth year (we keep only the year, from the date of birth you enter), school, city and state, sport, class year, and the name and email or phone of a coach or school contact. Players under 18 also give a parent or guardian's name, relationship, email and phone, and the guardian's agreement.
  • Coaches give their name, school, title, the sports they recruit for, a link to their school staff page and their school email address.
  • The reference contact, the guardian details and the coach's email are seen only by the administrators who review requests. They are never shown to other players or coaches.
  • A player's profile (height, weight, position, grades, test scores, stats, highlight link, bio, status and listed offers) is visible only to approved coaches who recruit that player's sport, and only while the player has turned the profile on. Players can turn it off at any time.
  • Coaches and players talk through messages on this site. Email addresses, phone numbers and links are blocked in messages. Messages are private to the two people, except that an administrator can read a conversation that is reported.
  • You can delete your recruiting account, request, profile and messages yourself from the Account tab of the Recruiting page. Your Fan Talk account, if you have one, is not affected.

Players must be at least 13. Parents and guardians of players under 18 can contact the site owner to review or delete their child's recruiting information.

Cookies

Logging in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is used for nothing else.

Chat messages

Messages are visible to other signed-in fans in the room. Reported messages can be read by moderators. Messages are kept until they age out of the chat history or a moderator removes them.

Children

Fan Talk is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Deleting your data

To have your account and messages removed, contact the site owner.

Third parties

Game data is provided through our server. The site doesn't use advertising or tracking scripts.
