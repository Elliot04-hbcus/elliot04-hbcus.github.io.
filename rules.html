Community rules

Fan Talk is for fans of SWAC and MEAC sports. Talk trash about the other team's offense, not about people.

Please do

  • Be respectful, even in a rivalry.
  • Keep it about the games, the teams and the culture around them.
  • Use Report when something breaks these rules, and Block if you don't want to see someone.

Please don't

  • Harass, threaten or bully anyone, or use hate speech.
  • Share personal information about yourself or anyone else, such as phone numbers, email addresses or home addresses.
  • Post links, spam or ads.
  • Pretend to be someone else, including players, coaches or staff.
  • Post anything sexual.

What happens if rules are broken

Messages that get 3 reports are hidden until a moderator reviews them. Moderators can delete messages and suspend accounts.

Contact

Questions, problems, or something you want removed: the site owner.
