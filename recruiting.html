Recruiting

This area is only for verified players and college coaches from SWAC and MEAC schools. It has its own account, separate from Fan Talk, and every account is reviewed by hand before it can be used.

Use an email only you can read. Coaches never see it. Players under 18: a parent or guardian helps with the next step.

Reset your password

Enter the recovery code you were shown when you created your account.

Save your recovery code

If you forget your password, this code is the only way to get back in, because we don't send emails yet. It is shown once. Screenshot it or write it down somewhere safe.

Request access

Recruiting is locked. Tell us who you are, and a person at HBCU Surge will review your request. You can use the recruiting tools only after it is approved. Reviews can take a few days.

Your email address and phone number are never shown to coaches. Coaches contact you only through messages on this site, and only if you turn messaging on.

Someone who can confirm you play

Your current coach or athletic director. Only the reviewers see this. It is used to check that you are a real player.

Parent or guardian (required under 18)

Players under 18 need a parent or guardian to agree. Only the reviewers see this, and we may contact them before approving.
